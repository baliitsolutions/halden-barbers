FAQ

What if I'm running late?

Call us. Up to 10 minutes late we'll still do the full service; after that we may need to shorten it or move you to the next free slot.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes, reply to your confirmation text or call us at least 2 hours before. Late cancellations twice in a row mean we'll ask for a deposit next time.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, any age. Kids' cuts are cheaper on weekdays before 3pm, when it's quieter.

Do you do curly and textured hair?

Absolutely. All three barbers cut every hair type, and Marcus specialises in curls and textured fades.
